# Payout operations

ClearLedger accepts payout requests from finance administrators and treasury
operators. Payouts above the automatic threshold require a finance
administrator before release.

Every production payout must retain:

- the initiating role;
- the amount and currency;
- the policy decision;
- whether a second approver was required.

Changes to payout limits, role boundaries, settlement precision, or audit
requirements must receive the owner review declared in the project policy.
